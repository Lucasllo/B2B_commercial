`OrderResponse` (`05-03`): `id, companyId, status, total, createdBy, createdAt, decidedBy, decidedAt,
reason, cancellationCode, cancellationReason, confirmedAt, cancelledAt, items`. Estados: `CREATED`

`REJECTED`, `RESERVING`, `CONFIRMED`, `CANCELLED` (`SHIPPED`/`DELIVERED` na Fase 6). Códigos de
cancelamento: `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`, `PRODUCT_NOT_STOCKED`, `RESERVATION_CANCELLED`,
`RESERVATION_TIMEOUT`. Exposição de crédito: APPROVED, RESERVING, CONFIRMED, SHIPPED, DELIVERED.

Estoque: `GET /api/inventory/{productId}` → `productId, quantityOnHand, quantityReserved,
quantityAvailable`; `PUT /api/inventory/{productId}` `{"quantityOnHand":n}` (SELLER_ADMIN).
Histórico: `GET /api/notifications/{productId}` (SELLER_ADMIN) com eventos `STOCK_ADJUSTED`.

Filas: `inventory-commands-queue`/`inventory-commands-dlq`, `order-events-queue`/`order-events-dlq`
(`maxReceiveCount` 3), `notification-events-queue` (sem DLQ). Outbox: tabelas `"order".outbox_event`
e `inventory.outbox_event` (`published_at`, `attempts`, `last_error`). Prazo da saga: 2 min
(`orderflow.saga.reservation-timeout`). Comando único do E2E: `./mvnw -B -pl e2e-tests -am verify`.

`E2E_CONFIG_STRATEGY`.
